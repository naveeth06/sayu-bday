November 4

Chapter three

Almost six months, and all the little things.

Midnight roads, a secret at the office, and a hundred quiet ways of saying what we have not said out loud. This is our little world, still becoming.

The timeline of us

  1. ✦

    Almost six months

    A little time. A whole lot of us.

    It may not sound like a lifetime, but these almost six months have given me moments I would not trade for anything. Somewhere along the way, the small things became our things: familiar, meaningful, and quietly precious to me.

  2. ☾

    After the world gets quiet

    Our little midnight adventures

    A few late drives, quiet roads, and the kind of conversations that feel different after midnight. I love the streetlights slipping by and the simple feeling of being there with you, with nowhere else we need to be for a little while.

  3. ✦

    Our very unofficial office secret

    Just friends, if anyone asks.

    Around our office friends, we do our best impression of two completely ordinary friends. The innocent smiles are convincing; the little world behind them is ours.

    To the world: just friends. Between us: so much more.

  4. ♡

    In your own time

    Three little words, in a hundred little ways.

    You have not said “I love you” to me yet, and that is okay. I never want those words to feel like a test or a deadline. I find them in the ways you show up, in the care you give, and in all the small things that sound like you.

    I will cherish every way you choose to show me love.

  5. ∞

    A future, taking its time

    Maybe our little beginning becomes forever.

    Our parents have started talking about marriage, and it makes the future feel a little less like a faraway thought. No need to rush the pages ahead: I just love imagining more drives, more ordinary days, and all the adventures we have not met yet.

Almost six months of little secrets,
midnight drives, unspoken words,
and a future we are still dreaming.

Still becoming

Our story is small so far. It already means so much.

Somewhere between the midnight roads, the secret smiles, and all the ways you make me feel loved, I have started picturing the pages ahead. Maybe one day these little memories will feel like the beginning of our forever. For now, I am happy that this is ours, one day at a time.
